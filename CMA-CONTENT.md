### What to Bring *(Under Review)*
- **Comfortable Clothes:** Wear comfy play clothes and closed-toe shoes.
- **Change of Clothes:** While we provide aprons and ponchos for paint and water play, you may want a fresh change of clothes.
- **Water Bottle:** Water bottles are allowed on the exhibit floor and can be refilled at our water fountains.
- **Membership Card and Tickets:** Bring your digital membership card and/or tickets to show at check-in.

### Food & Amenities *(Under Review)*
You can buy snacks and beverages in the Museum store or bring your own from home. Snacks can be eaten in the Snack Stop (next to the Museum Store). Water bottles and soda bottles with caps are permitted on the exhibit floor. There is a water fountain by the front entrance restrooms.

The Museum is walking-distance from several restaurants, including Mellow Mushroom, Waffle House, the restaurants in Peachtree Center, Kwan's Deli and Korean Kitchen, Baja Fresh and Chick-fil-a.

**Amenities:**
- **Coat Racks:** Available in the Museum for storing coats and jackets.
- **Lockers:** Small lockers can be checked-out at the front desk to store your items.
- **Sensory Packs:** Available at the front desk, checked out for free at any time. Each pack includes headphones and several sensory toys.
- **Stroller Parking:** Limited stroller parking available. CMA is not responsible for any valuable items lost or stolen. The Museum is fully stroller-accessible.
- **Quiet Room:** Located in Leaping into Learning. Available for families needing a quiet break or for nursing parents. Nursing is permitted anywhere in the Museum.

### Safety & Cleanliness *(Under Review)*
You are responsible for supervising your children at all times. Security cameras record everyone entering and leaving public entrances, as well as moving about the Museum in public spaces. Adults are not allowed to enter the Museum without a child. Museum team members are in radio communication with one another. When children are separated from their grown-ups, we immediately activate our reunification procedure. The Museum partners with a professional cleaning company; we use hospital-grade cleaning agents in the evening.

### Accessibility *(Under Review)*
- **ADA Compliance:** Contact Natalia Vuley at 404-507-7222 seven days in advance for reasonable accommodations. TDD/TTY: dial 711.
- **Wheelchair Accessibility:** The entire museum is wheelchair accessible. Elevators provide access to the Mezzanine. All bathrooms and water fountains are wheelchair accessible. The front entrance is wheelchair-accessible with automatic doors (red buttons, inside and outside).
- **Sensory Packs:** Available at the front desk, free to check out.
- **Sensory Friendly Playtime:** Offered on select Sunday mornings — limited admission and sound adjustments.
- **Service Animals:** Restrained service animals are permitted.
- **Web Accessibility:** Uses the Recite Me toolbar — text-to-speech, display customization, and translation to 134 languages.

### Membership Reciprocity *(Under Review)*
If you are a member at another children's museum with ACM reciprocal benefits, you are eligible to receive 50% off general admission for up to six people. A cardholding member must be present. Use promo code **ACMTIX** at checkout. A valid reciprocal membership card and picture ID will be required upon arrival.
- Link: https://findachildrensmuseum.org/reciprocal-network/

### Duration of Visit *(Under Review)*
Most families play for about 2 hours. Your tickets include re-entry on the day of your visit, as capacity allows.

### Code of Conduct *(Assigned)*
- **We Exercise Kindness:** Please use positive, encouraging behavior. Avoid profane and explicit language, harassment, and aggressive behavior.
- **We Engage In Respectful Behavior:** Please share and take turns. Treat the exhibits well so that other guests have a chance to experience them.
- **We Assume Good Intentions:** Children may unintentionally touch or bump into each other. Please be patient and understanding.
- **Stay Together, Play Together:** Adults must stay with their children at all times.
- **Use Walking Feet:** No running, please.
- **Make Memories, Don't Miss Them:** We encourage a screen-free experience, unless capturing precious memories.

### What to Wear *(Assigned)*
We recommend wearing comfortable "play clothes" and rubber-soled sneakers or shoes since children will be very active. Open-toe sandals, flip-flops, and high heels are strongly discouraged. Shoes must be worn at all times. Consider bringing a change of clothes. Coat racks are available in the Museum.

### Arrival Window *(Assigned)*
When purchasing/reserving your tickets online, you will need to select the hour block in which you want to arrive. If you arrive earlier or later than your arrival window, you may be asked to wait if the Museum is already at capacity.

### Policies & Rules *(Assigned)*
Children's Museum of Atlanta is designed to be enjoyed by families with young children. Exhibits and educational content are planned to be appropriate for children 8 and younger.

**Key Policies:**
- **Adult Supervision:** 1 adult chaperone required for every 5 children. Adults must supervise children at all times.
- **Animals:** Service animals only permitted (not pets, emotional support, or therapy animals).
- **Food, Snacks, and Drinks:** Food and snacks only in Snack Stop area. Water and soda bottles with caps allowed on the exhibit floor.
- **Inappropriate Language/Clothing:** Swearing and inappropriate language not permitted. Guests must wear proper attire.
- **Party Celebrations:** Museum party packages only; no outside party items permitted.
- **Photography:** Families may photograph their own family members only. By entering the facility, you consent to CMA using your image for marketing. Notify security immediately upon entering to opt out.
- **Re-entry:** Re-entry permitted if Museum has not reached capacity. Get your hand stamped at security before exiting.
- **Refunds:** General Admission tickets are non-refundable; unused tickets may be rescheduled within 90 days. Event and group tickets are non-refundable. Memberships are non-refundable and non-transferable.
- **Sick Policy:** Guests with elevated temperatures, contagious illness, or who are too sick to go to school/work should not visit.
- **Unaccompanied Adults:** No adult is permitted on the Museum floor without an accompanying child.
- **Unaccompanied Children:** Children may not enter or remain without an accompanying adult (18+).

**Prohibited Items:**
Alcohol, Balloons, Bicycles/Scooters, Cake/Cupcakes, Cigarettes/E-cigarettes, Coolers, Controlled substances, Costume Characters, Food, Gifts, Professional Photographers, Weapons (exception: uniformed police officer)

## Page: Field Trips

### Header: Field Trips

### Overview (Accordion)
**Field Trips Information:**
Self-guided field trips offer an opportunity for your group to explore the Museum at their own pace through chaperone-led play.

**Field Trip Includes:**
- A welcome and brief orientation by a Museum Educator
- Opportunity to explore the Museum's Learning Zones
- Experiencing our Super Spectacular Science Show on stage
- Chaperone responsibilities document and field trip flow guide

*Due to space and time constraints, field trips do not participate in the Art Studio and Science Bar.*

### Request a Visit
Field trips are all self-guided visits with a 9:30 am arrival time and a field trip time of 10 am – 12 pm. *Please note that our online booking form does not guarantee a reservation — our team will reach out to you to verify your request. A deposit is required in order to confirm your reservation.*

### Field Trip Details (Accordion)
Field trips are scheduled for 10 am – 12 pm on Tuesdays, Thursdays, Fridays, and select Mondays and Wednesdays. Check-in is at 9:30 am. Maximum Field Trip capacity is 250 people (this includes students, teachers and adult chaperones). The Museum requires one (1) adult for every five (5) children. Required adults are free and counted towards the 250 capacity. Additional adult chaperones MUST be paid through the school and cannot purchase individual tickets online.

**Lunch Space:** Two rooms available on a first come, first serve basis. Maximum 36 total people per room (30 students and 6 adults). Both rooms may be booked if availability allows and must be booked at time of reservation. Each room may be booked two times maximum per visit (120 student capacity). Alternatively, school groups are welcome to walk across to Centennial Olympic Park for picnic lunch.

### Learning Zones (Accordion)
**Permanent Exhibits:**
- **Gateway to the World:** Travel the world and explore six continents and climb among the planets.
- **Fundamentally Food:** Follow the food we eat from farm to table. Milk Buttercup, the Museum's cow, and pick vegetables from a garden. Ship the harvest to the local store. Create a nutritious meal at the Museum's pretend diner.
- **Let Your Creativity Flow:** Explore the science of color at the paint wall and build topographical landscapes at the augmented reality sand table.
- **Tools for Solutions:** Learn about the six simple machines with the giant ball machine. Explore electricity and connect pipes at the construction house. Design and test a structure against earthquakes.
- **Step Up to Science:** Learn about the science of light and sound in Catch a Wave. Also offers opportunities to explore engineering in Jane's Innovation Station.

*Additional learning zone for kindergarten and younger:*
- **Leaping into Learning:** Catch a colorful fish at our pond, go under our waterfall without getting wet. Climb into our tree house and see animals from the forest.

Explore our grade-by-grade standards alignment and discover how our field trips support GELDS and Georgia Standards of Excellence.

### Field Trip+ (Accordion)

### Field Trip FAQs

**Booking & Deposits:**
Space is limited and dates go quickly, so reserve early! A 50% non-refundable credit card or check deposit is required within two weeks of booking. Have at least three possible dates and the number of students and adults (1 adult for every 5 students required). Final payment is due on the day of the visit. Call Reservations at 404.527.3693, email reservations@childrensmuseumatlanta.org, or fill out the online request form.

**Cancellations:**
Call as soon as possible to change a reservation. Must cancel within six (6) weeks of the scheduled program to apply the deposit to a different date within the same school year. Cancellations made less than 6 weeks prior will result in forfeiture of 50% deposit.

**Chaperones:**
One chaperone required for every five students. Required chaperones are free at the 1:5 ratio. Additional adult chaperones must be reserved/paid through the school for $15.95 + tax per person.

**Capacity:**
Maximum field trip capacity is 250 people. One adult for every 5 students required. Minimum 10 students to qualify for field trip pricing.

**Lunch:**
Two rooms available at $50 per room. Max 36 people per room (30 students + 6 chaperones). Up to 4 lunch sessions per visit. Centennial Olympic Park is also a popular picnic destination.

**Payment:**
School checks (to Children's Museum of Atlanta), cash, or credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express), or Bill.com. School check or school credit card must be used for tax exempt status.

**Parking:**
Full-size buses can park at Georgia World Congress Center Marshalling Yard (fee; purchase online through GWCC). Pre-purchase parking at 236 Williams Street (one block away) recommended for additional adults.

**Sensory Packs:**
Limited quantity available. Include headphones, sunglasses, and various fidget toys. Contact Reservations to reserve.

**Target Ages:**
Preschool to third grade. For 4th and 5th grade, see Museum On-the-Go programs.

**Tax Exempt:**
Provide Tax Exemption Certificate to Reservations Department prior to deposit payment.
